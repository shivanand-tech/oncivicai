How CivicAI Powers Urban Resolution

No endless bureaucratic black-holes. Every submission undergoes automated computer triage and transparent SLA accountability.

1

Snap & Geotag

Citizens take a photo and pin their location. SmartVision AI detects features (pothole cavity, exposed wires, garbage debris) and predicts category automatically.

2

AI Priority & Deduplication

The engine computes an explainable 0-100 score based on hazard severity and footfall. Duplicate Radar checks nearby radius to prevent redundant crew dispatch.

3

Department Routing & SLA

Automatically routes to PWD, SWM, BESCOM or BWSSB with an executive action brief. A strict SLA clock counts down to target resolution.

4

Citizen Verification & Rating

Engineers attach photographic proof upon completion. Citizens verify the fix with a 5-star rating, closing the feedback loop.
